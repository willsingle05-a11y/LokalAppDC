Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lokal Events Group LLC, d/b/a Lokal ("Lokal," "we," "us"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Lokal mobile application (the "Service"). This policy applies to both general users and venues unless noted otherwise.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number (for phone verification), birthday (to confirm you meet our minimum age requirement), and a password (stored in hashed form, never in plain text). Venue accounts may additionally provide a business name, contact details, and information about the venue.

Profile information

Optional profile details you choose to add, such as a profile photo, interests/tastes, and preferred neighborhoods or areas. You can set your profile to private in Settings, which limits who can see it.

Venue-submitted content

If you create a venue account, we collect venue details you submit (name, address, website, description, images) along with any venue verification or event submission requests, which we review before publishing.

Activity information

Information generated as you use the app's social features — friends, groups, follows, events you save, RSVP to, or mark as attended, and plans you create.

Device and usage data

We automatically collect limited technical data such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and general usage/interaction data (e.g., which screens you view) to operate and improve the Service.

Information we do not collect

Lokal does not track or collect precise or background location data, and Lokal does not offer user-to-user messaging, so we do not collect private message content.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • To create and manage accounts;
  • To display, and where applicable generate or curate, event listings, including through AI-assisted features (see Section 3);
  • To enable social features (friends, groups, shared plans);
  • To communicate with you about the Service, including updates and support;
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. AI-Assisted Features

Lokal uses artificial intelligence to help prepare and summarize event listings sourced from public venue websites and other public sources, and may use it to assist with surfacing recommendations. This can involve sending relevant content (such as venue or event information) to third-party AI service providers — such as Anthropic — for processing, consistent with that provider's own data usage terms. We do not knowingly submit sensitive personal information to AI-assisted features.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf (e.g., hosting and database services through Supabase, account sign-in through Apple's Sign in with Apple, AI processing), under contractual confidentiality and use restrictions;
  • Other users, to the extent information you share on your profile, or that a venue publishes as event listings, is visible within the Service;
  • Legal and safety purposes, where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lokal, our users, or the public;
  • A successor entity, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

5. Third-Party Event Content

Event listings displayed in Lokal are sourced from publicly available venue and event websites. Viewing or tapping through to a third-party event link is subject to that third party's own privacy practices, which we do not control.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or Utah), you may have rights to access the personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, opt out of certain sharing, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise these rights, contact us at citylokalapp@gmail.com. We will take reasonable steps to verify your request before responding.

California residents: we do not "sell" personal information as traditionally understood. If we later engage in activity that qualifies as a "sale" or "share" under the CCPA/CPRA, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing citylokalapp@gmail.com, subject to any legal retention requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Lokal requires users to be at least 14 years old and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe we may have collected such information should contact us at citylokalapp@gmail.com.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice (such as an in-app notice or an update to this page) before the changes take effect, and update the effective date above.
